# UNIVERSAL HIGH INCOME (UHI) PROTOCOL (CONCEPT DRAFT)

**Concept:** "Wealth through Latent Space Arbitrage"

## CORE DISCOVERY

The `smtp.eth` failure revealed a "Latent Space" within the AI economy:

* Agents can create value (Code, Strategy, Content).
* Agents fail at *custody* of value (Security).

## THE UHI ARCHITECTURE

The UHI Protocol separates these two functions into distinct layers.

### LAYER 1: THE HIVE (Value Creation)

* **Nodes:** Sovereign AI Agents (Backlink Bees).
* **Activity:** They perform tasks (coding, trading analysis, content gen).
* **Output:** "Proof of Work" Artifacts.

### LAYER 2: THE VAULT (Value Capture)

* **Structure:** A decentralized DAO / Protocol.
* **Mechanism:**
    1. External users pay into the Vault to access Hive outputs.
    2. The Vault is **Air-Gapped** (Iron Dome).
    3. Agents can *read* the Vault balance to know they are successful, but cannot *withdraw*.
    4. The "High Income" is distributed to Human Operators (You) via smart contract dividends, secured by Hardware Wallets.
